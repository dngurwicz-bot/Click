## Market Anchors

### CLICK Core
- Gusto Simple: `$49/mo + $6/user/mo` for SMB payroll and HR.
- Gusto Plus: `$80/mo + $12/user/mo` for advanced payroll, HR, and attendance.
- BambooHR pricing page is public, but pricing is still quote-based.

Recommendation note:
- Core was priced below the market floor for HR + payroll. The new recommendation keeps onboarding accessible but stops underpricing the main platform foundation.

### CLICK Flow
- monday.com Standard: `€12/seat/month`.
- monday.com Pro: `€19/seat/month`.

Recommendation note:
- Workflow products are materially more expensive than the current CLICK Flow pricing. The recommendation stays under monday Standard at 10 users while reflecting automation value.

### CLICK Docs
- PandaDoc Starter: `$19/seat/month`.
- PandaDoc Business: `$49/seat/month`.

Recommendation note:
- Even with an HR-docs framing rather than sales CPQ, the current pricing is too low relative to document workflow and approval tooling.

### CLICK Vision
- Workleap Performance: `$5/user/month`.
- Lattice Performance / Goals: `$8-$11/seat/month` depending on package.

Recommendation note:
- Vision should remain cheaper than Insights and behave like a visibility/performance layer rather than an advanced analytics product.

### CLICK Assets
- Sortly Advanced: `$24/mo` for 2 licenses.
- Sortly Ultra: `$74/mo` for 5 licenses.
- Sortly Premium: `$149/mo` for 8 licenses.

Recommendation note:
- Assets should stay primarily base-priced, but the current fixed price is too low for a real operational asset tool.

### CLICK Vibe
- Workleap Officevibe: `$5/user/month`.
- Lattice Engagement: `$4/seat/month`.

Recommendation note:
- Engagement products usually price lower than performance-development suites. Vibe should stay below Grow to keep bundle logic clean.

### CLICK Grow
- Lattice Grow: `$4/seat/month`.
- 15Five Perform: `$11/user/month`.

Recommendation note:
- Grow should price above Vibe and below a full talent suite. The current price is too close to premium HR suite territory.

### CLICK Insights
- Microsoft Power BI Pro: `$14/user/month`.
- Microsoft Power BI Premium Per User: `$24/user/month`.

Recommendation note:
- Insights should remain the highest-priced analytics module, but the current 10-user effective total is too close to the top end of the category.
